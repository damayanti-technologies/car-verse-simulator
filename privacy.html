1. Introduction

Welcome to CarVerse Simulator, a multiplayer car racing game developed and published by Damayanti Technologies.

We respect the privacy of our players and are committed to protecting their personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is stored, and what choices users have when playing CarVerse Simulator.

CarVerse Simulator is designed to be suitable for users of all ages, including children. We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide the game's features and services.

By using CarVerse Simulator, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide the game's functionality, we may collect the following information:

A. Account Information

Depending on the login method selected, we may process:

  • Firebase-generated user ID.
  • Email address, when provided through email or Google login.
  • Authentication and account-related information.
  • Display name or profile information, where provided through the selected sign-in method.

Guest users may use the game without providing an email address.

B. Player Profile Information

We may collect and store:

  • Player username.
  • Player ID.
  • Profile information.
  • Game progress.
  • Virtual coin balance.
  • Racing statistics and achievements.

C. Multiplayer Information

To provide online multiplayer functionality, we may process:

  • Player IDs and usernames.
  • Friend requests and friend relationships.
  • Multiplayer room information.
  • Race-related information.
  • Game progress and results.
  • Information necessary to synchronize multiplayer gameplay.

Some profile information, such as usernames and racing statistics, may be visible to other players through multiplayer and social features.

D. Technical Information

Our service providers may process limited technical information necessary to authenticate users, operate the game, maintain security and troubleshoot technical problems.

This may include connection information, IP addresses and technical service logs processed by Firebase or its underlying infrastructure.

We do not intentionally collect precise location information, contact lists, microphone recordings or camera information through CarVerse Simulator.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described in this policy for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing player accounts.
  • Providing guest, Google and email authentication.
  • Managing player profiles and usernames.
  • Enabling online multiplayer racing.
  • Managing friends and friend requests.
  • Saving player progress and virtual coin balances.
  • Displaying racing statistics and achievements.
  • Maintaining game functionality.
  • Preventing misuse and unauthorized access.
  • Identifying and resolving technical problems.
  • Improving game performance and user experience.
  • Providing promotional information about Damayanti Technologies' own games, applications and products.

We do not sell players' personal information.

4. Firebase Services

CarVerse Simulator uses Google Firebase services to provide essential game functionality.

The Firebase services currently used include:

Firebase Authentication

Used to:

  • Register and authenticate players.
  • Support guest login.
  • Support Google Sign-In.
  • Support email and password login.
  • Manage account authentication.

Firebase Realtime Database

Used to:

  • Store and synchronize player profiles.
  • Manage usernames and player IDs.
  • Manage friends and friend requests.
  • Store relevant multiplayer and game-related information.
  • Maintain player progress and virtual game data.

Firebase is a service provided by Google. Information processed through Firebase is also subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Advertising and Promotions

CarVerse Simulator may display promotional content relating exclusively to products, applications, games and services developed or offered by Damayanti Technologies.

These promotions are intended to inform players about our own products and services.

Our current advertising approach does not use third-party advertising networks or third-party advertising SDKs.

We do not intentionally use personalized advertising, behavioral advertising or third-party advertising trackers in the game.

Promotional content will be designed to be appropriate for the game's intended audience, including children.

Promotional content will not intentionally mislead users or encourage accidental interactions.

6. Virtual Coins and In-Game Items

CarVerse Simulator includes a virtual currency system.

Players may use virtual coins to obtain available in-game items, vehicles, upgrades or other game features.

Important information:

  • Virtual coins are in-game digital units.
  • Virtual coins are not real money.
  • Virtual coins cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • The game does not currently offer real-money purchases of virtual coins.
  • The game does not require payment information or banking details to obtain or use virtual coins.
  • Virtual coins and game items are subject to the game's functionality and applicable game rules.

7. Children's Privacy

CarVerse Simulator is designed to be suitable for users of all ages, including children.

We understand the importance of protecting children's privacy and take reasonable measures to limit unnecessary information collection.

Our intended practices include:

  • Not requesting microphone access.
  • Not requesting camera access.

  • Not providing voice chat.

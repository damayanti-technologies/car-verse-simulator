Damayanti Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") provides this Privacy Policy to inform you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our mobile application, Car Verse Simulator.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to operate the game, maintain online multiplayer synchronization, and provide a secure, enjoyable gaming experience:

Data Category Data Types Purpose
Account & Identity Email address, Display Name, User ID (UID), Google Sign-In profile information. Account authentication, persistent garage storage, and career progression saving via Firebase Authentication.
Gameplay & Telemetry Vehicle coordinates, lap times, drift scores, leaderboard rankings, and online race room status. Real-time multiplayer race synchronization and global competitive leaderboards.
Device Diagnostics Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, GPU specifications (Vulkan / OpenGL ES capabilities), and anonymized crash logs. Engine optimization, frame-rate pacing, and application stability improvements.

Guest Mode: Players may choose to play without signing in. In Guest Mode, player data is tied only to a temporary anonymous identifier on the device.

3. How We Use Collected Data

We process collected data exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To authenticate player credentials and restore saved game progress across sessions.
  • To host and coordinate real-time online multiplayer races and update live leaderboards.
  • To identify hardware-specific graphics glitches, engine bottlenecks, or crash instances.
  • To enforce game integrity and prevent unauthorized cheating or exploits in multiplayer races.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

Car Verse Simulator integrates with trusted third-party service providers that maintain strict industry data protection standards:

  • Google Play Services: Provides licensing, in-app billing verification, and application updates. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Authentication & Infrastructure (Google LLC): Used for identity management, token verification, and cloud database communication. Firebase Privacy Overview.

5. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical and administrative security controls. All communication between the game client and servers is encrypted in transit using modern HTTPS and Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protocols.

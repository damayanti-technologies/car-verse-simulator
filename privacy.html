2. Children's Privacy & Family-Friendly Commitment (All Ages)

Protected Family Experience: Car Verse Simulator does not require children to disclose personal contact information to play. The game is completely accessible via Guest Mode with zero personal data collection.

Our commitments for younger players, children, and families include:

  • No Mandatory Personal Information: Children can enjoy all racing tracks, single-player modes, and vehicle customizations without creating an account or providing an email address.
  • Support for Internal Operations Only: Any technical identifiers collected (such as temporary anonymous device tokens or graphics capability flags) are used strictly to support the internal technical operations of the game, such as graphics rendering, frame pacing, and local save storage.
  • No Behavioral Tracking or Profiling: We do not track, profile, or target behavioral advertisements to children or any player.
  • Parental Rights: Parents and legal guardians have the full right to review, update, or request the immediate permanent deletion of any data associated with their child. To submit a parental request, email us directly at damayanti.technologies@gmail.com.

3. Information We Collect

We collect limited information to operate the game, maintain online multiplayer synchronization, and provide a secure, enjoyable gaming experience:

Data Category Data Types Purpose
Account & Identity (Optional) Email address, Display Name, and User ID (UID) if the player voluntarily signs in via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication. Cross-device garage backup, profile recovery, and career save restoration. Optional for all players.
Gameplay & Telemetry Vehicle coordinates, lap times, drift scores, leaderboard placements, and race room session state. Real-time multiplayer race synchronization and fair global leaderboards.
Device Diagnostics Device manufacturer, model, OS version, GPU hardware specs (Vulkan / OpenGL ES support), and anonymized crash logs. 3D engine optimization, graphic compatibility across devices, and crash prevention.

Guest Mode: In Guest Mode, all data is stored locally on the device or tied only to a temporary anonymous identifier that contains no personal information.

4. How We Use Collected Data

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To authenticate player credentials and restore saved game progress across sessions.
  • To coordinate real-time online multiplayer races and update live race room leaderboards.
  • To identify hardware-specific graphics bugs, engine bottlenecks, or crash instances.
  • To maintain fair play and protect multiplayer races from unauthorized cheats or exploits.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

5. Third-Party Services

Car Verse Simulator integrates with certified third-party service providers that comply with Google Play Families and data security standards:

All communications between the game client and server infrastructure are encrypted in transit using modern HTTPS and Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protocols. We maintain strict technical safeguards to protect player records from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

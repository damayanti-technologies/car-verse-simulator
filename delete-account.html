1. Policy Overview

Under the Google Play Developer Policy on Account Deletion, players, parents, and legal guardians of Car Verse Simulator have the right to request the complete deletion of their account credentials, personal data, and gameplay records.

You can delete your account using either of the following two options:

  • In-App Deletion: Instantly delete your account and records from within the game settings.
  • Email / Web Request: Submit an account deletion request if you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your Android device.

2. Method 1: Delete Account Inside the App

If you have Car Verse Simulator installed on your device, follow these steps:

Step 1: Open Settings

Launch Car Verse Simulator and tap the Settings (gear icon) on the top-right corner of the screen.

Step 2: Select Account Deletion

In the Settings menu, select Delete Account (Google Play Policy).

Step 3: Confirm Deletion

A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap Yes, Permanently Delete. Your Firebase credentials, garage progress, and telemetry will be removed immediately.

3. Method 2: Request Account Deletion by Email (Parents & Players)

If you have uninstalled the app, are requesting deletion on behalf of your child, or prefer to request deletion via email, click the button below to generate a pre-formatted email request to our support team:

Send Account Deletion Request Email

You can also send an email manually using the following format:

To: damayanti.technologies@gmail.com Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request - Car Verse Simulator Body: Hello Damayanti Technologies Support, Please permanently delete my game account and all associated data for Car Verse Simulator. My Account Information: - Registered Email Address: [Your Email] - In-Game Driver Name or User ID (if known): [Your Name or UID] Thank you.

4. What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Data that is permanently deleted:

  • Firebase Authentication profile, email address, password hash, and User ID.
  • Saved garage progress, vehicle setups, and career milestones.
  • Online multiplayer leaderboard placements, lap times, and telemetry.

Data that may be retained (legal requirement only):

  • Statutory Financial Records: In-app purchase transaction receipts (if any) are retained only where mandated by statutory tax, accounting, or anti-fraud laws. No personal gameplay or login data is retained.

5. Deletion Timeline Within 30 Days

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • In-app deletion: Executed immediately upon confirmation.
  • Email requests: Verified and permanently processed within 30 calendar days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.
